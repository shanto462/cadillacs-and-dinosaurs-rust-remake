# M3-07: snapshots of later episodes and areas, for the port's snapshot loader (dino-game --load-state / --step-from).
# Each scenario jumps into an episode and area at a camera X, takes the snapshot and records 600 game frames of walking.
# Record: PR_SNAPS=none PR_SHEET=0 re/harness/probe/probe_record.sh re/harness/pokes/m307_snapshots.txt all
# Run:    MODE=step re/harness/port.sh re/out/traces/NAME.input.txt NAME 600   (picks up NAME.pokes.txt, the god writes)
# `snapshot keep` keeps `god on` running: MAME rewrites +$00 and +$F9 of the players every frame, and the sidecar
# NAME.pokes.txt makes the port do the same. The scenarios without `keep` need no sidecar writes.

# Episode 2 (stage 1), area 0, camera X 0x400: the check of M3-07. Jack walks right with a few attacks and jumps.
segment m307_e2a0_x400_walk
jump 2 0 0x400 char=jack
god on
10 snapshot keep
0-99:R
100-101:A
110-111:A
120-199:R
200-201:J
202-299:R
300-301:A
302-399:R
400-401:J
402-599:R
trace 600

# The same without god mode: Jack can be hit, so no write of the probe is involved (the sidecar is empty).
