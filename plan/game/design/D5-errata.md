# D5 errata

Corrections to the design documents found while building (append only, one line per correction, newest last).
MAME wins over the design text; the design documents themselves are not edited.

- D4d s2 (boot skip): "press at 100: both screens end at 226" is wrong for text 1. A press at probe frame 100 ends the wait of text 1 early (text 2 starts at once), but text 2 still shows its full 30 + 91 frames from its early start, so the boot ends 121 frames after text 2 starts (video frame 102) and the layer control change comes 3 frames later, at video frame 226. Text 2 is only skipped by a press after its first 30 frames (boot-skip-b). M1-03.
- D4d s2, s3.2, s13 A2: frame numbers of the probe batches are video frames; a record of the harness traces is video frame - 2, and a press of a probe line `f` is seen one frame earlier by a task than the harness script line `f` (the same press gives the layer control change 0x18D6 at video frame 226 / 234 in the probe and at record 224 / 231 with `98-101:A` / `228-231:2J`). M1-03.
- D4d s4.5 and F35 (G.$4F6): the counter feeds nothing. The system task clears it (0x1827A with G.$4CC set) in the pass that creates the game task, two frames before the game start code 0x17F8A reads it, so the starting rank band is always 0; and with the factory settings (three chutes, credits per player) it never counts, because it only runs while the common pool G.$46A or free play G.$46B is not 0. M1-04.
- D4d s3.2 (attract timeline): the lengths of the profile cards (1399 frames the first time, 1407 afterwards), of the title entry and of the ranking (422 then 423) are not constant; the whole loop is 13104 frames only on average. The measured lengths are in `attract.rs` and `TITLE_RUNS` in `boot.rs`. M1-04.

- D4d s3.4 and D5 M1-05 (scroll helper): "the helper 0x9AC86 moves the checker board (scroll2 X += 4 or 8, scroll3 X += 4)" is wrong, and 0x9AC86 / 0x9ACBA are code, not tables. Both helpers act only when G.-$7014 or G.-$7012 is not 0, and no code in the ROM ever sets them (their only other uses are the clears at 0x9A818 and 0x9A832), so they do nothing. The board moves by the pool B class 94 object (scroll3 Y, G.$7E8, minus 2 per update for 295 updates of each card; MAME: 65534, 65532 ... 64946); scroll2 and scroll3 X stay 0. M1-05.
- D4d s3.4, s15 item 1 and D5 M1-06 (the card objects): pool A class 100 variants 0 to 3 are the big portrait of the hero (an animation of the table 0x71F9E, OBJ palette 16 loaded from 0xBB252 / 0xBB3B2 / 0xBB292 / 0xBB3F2, X camera + 0x58, Mess + 0x50), variants 4 to 7 are the small hero who does the moves (X camera + 0x120, Y + 0x20). Their sounds are the `+$2D` byte of the animation frames, queued in every update the frame is shown. Pool B class 93 is the checker board (a tile writer for scroll3 that also touches the last 8 cells of scroll2; its first update clears scroll3 twice and so crosses a video frame boundary), class 94 the scroll of it. Class 100 lives 300 updates and class 94 lives 296 (class 93 never frees itself); the counter of the routine stops while a fade or the clear sleeps, so every card shows its objects the whole time. M1-06.
- D4d s3.4 (timing): the first profile run is one frame ahead of the later runs at 8 places (the first pass, the pass of counter 11, and for each of the cards 2 to 4 the end of the clear sleep and the second step of the fade in), which is the 8 frames between 1399 and 1407. The two timings are in `flow/profiles.rs`. The scroll registers of the hardware show the shadow G.$7E8 two frames late in the recording (cpsa 0x16: 0x700 minus the shadow of two records before), so a port picture that shows the shadow at once has the checker 4 pixels (2 frames at 2 pixels) away from the MAME picture while it scrolls. M1-05 and M1-06.
- D4d s3.5 (ranking objects): the routine creates more objects than the Cadillac. The set-up creates, before the pools are used, a cloud manager (pool B class 89, 0x8C982, made by 0x8C96C) that makes the 15 clouds of the start (pool C class 0x80, handler 0x96A7E, speeds 3.5, 2.5 and 1.5 pixels a frame to the right of the world, i.e. slower than the 8 pixel scroll) and then, every 0 to 15 frames by a random number, one new cloud 0x280 right of the camera at one of 3 x 8 heights. The Cadillac (pool B class 86) makes two heroes (pool A class 17, 0x423A4) that follow it and bob by one random number each a frame. So the ranking screen uses the random number generator on every frame (about 3 calls), and G.$14 after it is part of the timeline. M1-07.
- D4d s3.5 (ranking timing): the states 0 and 2 are one pass (the draw), G.$7018 = 0x1A4 counts down in 421 more passes to -1 (the 422nd pass sets state 6 and returns before the objects), the scroll steps (+8 scroll2, +4 scroll3) come after the handler and before the object updates, so the Cadillac (mode 0 does not move) has moved n - 1 times after n passes. The drawing of about 2250 cells is cut by the video frame boundary in the middle of a cell write: the first frame after the call has done 3958, 3054 or 3786 of the 4498 words (code word then attribute word of each cell), by the screen before the ranking (`flow/ranking.rs`, `CUTS`). The first run is again one frame ahead of the later ones (its second pass is in the frame of the first). M1-07.

## From M2-13 and M2-14 (stage clock and TIME OVER, 2026-10-13)

- D3f s3.2 (slot 15 address): "RAM 0xFFDFE4" is wrong. Pool D slot 15 is at 0xFFE574 (pool D base 0xDDF4 + 15 * 0x80; the stack is filled from slot 0 to slot 15 and popped from the low end, so slot 15 is the first to go). The slot is a byte image the clock fills (18 longs of the template 0x81194, which also carries the first 9 digit words of 0x811CA at +$36): the draw record at +$00 (a long pointer 0xFFFF0000 + slot + 0xA, because `movea.w` sign extends the address, then four parameter bytes), and the sprite list at +$0A (count, 5 sprites of dx, dy, attr, code). The tile codes of the three digits are at +$14, +$1C and +$24. The free routine 0x4994 does not clear the slot: the byte +$00 stays 0xFF after the clock ends. M: `re/harness/pokes/m213_clock.txt`, slot words equal in every frame. M2-13.
- D3f FX-S07 and D4b s1.4 (red): the digits turn red at 1:00 (G.$4E8 <= 0x0100, palette override 0x84), not at 0:15. At 0:15 only the steady display starts (window 800 frames).
