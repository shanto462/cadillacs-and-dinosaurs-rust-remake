# D3c: bosses part 1 (Vice T., the Butcher, Hogg with the Episode 3 car section, Slice)

Written 2026-10-12 by the D3c research agent. Output of task D3c of `plan/game/NEW-PLAN.md`. Nothing here is ROM data: only
descriptions, numbers and addresses.

Source tags: **R:** ROM address or repo file, **M:** MAME probe (the segment name is given; the generators are
`re/out/d3c/gen_r1.py` to `gen_r5.py`, the logs are in `re/out/probe/d3c-r1` to `d3c-r5`, all git-ignored), **W:** web,
**U:** the user. **MAME wins over the web.** `[I]` marks an inference that no run or listing proves. `G.$xxxx` is RAM
`0xFF8000 + xxxx`. Pool A classes are numbered by `+$20 / 4` (the number the scripts print as `A#nn`). Box records are
`zo, zs, xo, xw, yo, ys` (depth offset, depth size, x offset, x width, height offset, height size); the x offset is for
an object that faces left, a box is mirrored when it faces right. Frame numbers are game frames after the jump of the probe
(`t`), unless a text says "video frames". One second is 51 game frames on the clock but 60 video frames; all timings below are
`t` of the probe (video frames, no slow motion) unless marked `gf`.

How the work was done: I read the handlers of classes 5, 18, 25, 31, 32, 42, 61, 19 and of the pool B classes 22 and 88 with the
annotated listings in `re/out/d3c/cNN_ann.txt` (generated by `gen.sh` from `re/tools/d2`), decoded the animation tables and boxes
(`boxes.py`), decoded the reinforcement lists (`b22lists.py`, output `b22_lists.txt`), looked at the asset dump
(`re/out/assets/anims/boss_a*`) and ran **5 headless MAME batches (`probe.sh`)**: run 1 scouting (21 segments), run 2 targeted
(24 segments), run 3 (5 segments), run 4 (10 segments), run 5 (6 segments). Where a probe was weak (my first "face right"
bot hit the bosses too seldom) I say so and the numbers come from the better segment. Segment names are in the text.

### 1.3 What every boss shares in its object

- `+$7D = 0xFF`: the boss is not counted by the census (`G.$4F3`), so waves and the reinforcement controller do not wait for it,
  and kill-all pulses did not kill any of them (M: the `killall` pulses of v1 to v9, b10, h13 never touched a boss). [R, M]
- `+$6A = 100` (bar scale), `+$6C` HP, `+$6E` the HP before the last hit (the death test uses it), `+$C0`/`+$C8` initial HP or bar
  copies for the thresholds (Vice `+$C0` = max HP, Butcher `+$C8` = 100).
- The hit reaction state (`+$05 = 2`) always begins with 0x3262A (the damage dealt is added to the hero's `+$10C`, the "best
  player" statistic; in car mode it is added to **all three heroes**), and when the HP is 0 and `+$6E != 0` it calls 0x32740 (puts the
  boss back inside camera X .. camera X + 0x180), **0xACEE** (boss defeat), clears `+$6E` and calls **0xA5EA** (the music stop cue
  `0xFF07`, parameter `0x4440000`). [R 0x4385C Vice, 0x4C8C8 Slice, 0x50364 Hogg, 0x5A36A Butcher]
- The death state (`+$04 = 4`): kill score (0x325E6, the killer is `G.$4E0`), and 0x6148: clears the three heroes' `+$C0` flags and sets the
  leader flag of the killer (of **all three** heroes in car mode). [R 0x50760, 0x6148]
- Guarding: none of the four blocks. Poise (`+$78`) is reduced by the poise value of each hit; at 0 the reaction is the heavy one.
  Vice resets his poise to 11 `+$79` frames after the last hit; Hogg to 10 after 60 frames. [R]

### 1.7 The reinforcement controller (pool B class 22, R 0x7BEF6), what each boss uses

Created by the boss init (0x7BEDC: Vice 0x4286A (variant 0), Slice 0x4BFF4, Butcher 0x58604, Hogg 0x4FE40). The controller sets
`G.$7A9`... and calls 0x1D95E (the boss death script) when it sees the boss at HP 0. It has two lists per episode (tables
0x7BF36 and 0x7BF56, 8 entries); the full decode (every record: class, variant, X, Z, drop, player condition) is in
`re/out/d3c/b22_lists.txt`. Rules (R 0x7BF94, 0x7BFF2):

- **Timed list** (not in car mode): a record is spawned `delay` frames after the previous one, only while the number of counted
  enemies `G.$4F3` is **below 4 / 5 / 6** (1 / 2 / 3 players, table 0x7BFEE). Episode 1 (Vice): 5 records (a Gneiss after 480 for
  2+ players, Driver after 240 for 2+, Ferris after 600 and 0 for 3, a Ferris after 1800 for everyone). Episode 4 (Slice): 40 records of
  class 63 / 64 every 1800 to 2400 frames (the first two at level 31 or 3 players). Episodes 2 and 3: none.
- **Threshold list** (needs `G.$502 > 0`, `G.$518 = 0` and not stopped): the groups are used **in order**; group k is released
  when the count of counted enemies is **at or below** its three thresholds (1 / 2 / 3 players); each record of the group is spawned
  after its own delay; at the end `G.$502 = 0` and the controller waits for the next `G.$502`. An empty group (thresholds only) ends the
  list (`G.$502 = 0xFF`).
- Who sets `G.$502`: **Vice** at the end of every summon (so the groups come only after a summon); **Slice** every 300 frames (`+$C4`),
  **Hogg** every 300 frames (`+$C8`), the **Butcher** every 30 frames (`+$AA`, so his groups wait only for the thresholds).
- Spawner: stages 0, 1, 3 use 0xFDAE (X is an absolute map X: 0x1C0 / 0x370 / 0x260 / 0x410 are the left and right edge of the boss
  screen of Episode 1 and 2); stage 2 (Hogg) uses 0xFE6A: **X is relative to the camera** (0xFFD0 = 48 px left of the screen,
  0x190 = 400 px right of its left edge).

### 4.5 Car behaviour (R 0x500B2..0x5020A; M c1-base, c3-noinput, h7-ratio)

  16 px steps, 8 rows dz = +56, +40, +24, +8, -8, -24, -40, -56**); the cell is chosen by a stochastic map: `+$A8` = the cell the bike is in now relative to the car (`((car Z - bike Z + 0x40)
